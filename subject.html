1. Project Overview

Business idea: A secure enterprise platform for consulting teams or companies to manage projects, business documents, risks and AI-powered document analysis from one web application.

ProblemBusiness documents are difficult to organize, search and analyze manually.
UsersAdmins, managers, members and viewers.
Main valueCentral document management + permissions + AI answers + risk tracking.
TargetProduction-style portfolio project for software/technology internship interviews.

What a real user should be able to do

  • Create an account and log in.
  • See a dashboard with useful business metrics.
  • Create and manage projects.
  • Invite or manage project members.
  • Upload PDF, DOCX and TXT documents.
  • Search, filter and paginate documents.
  • View document metadata and activity.
  • Ask questions about uploaded documents.
  • See AI answers with document/page/section references when available.
  • Create and manage business risks.
  • View analytics and charts.
  • See audit history of important actions.
